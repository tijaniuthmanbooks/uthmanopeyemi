What I found

  • A warm, encouraging voice and a steady chapter rhythm that closes each time with “A Lesson for the Reader”.
  • The same idea told again: “Confidence comes after action” in Chapters 1, 3 and 7.
  • Habits of phrasing: “I began” 25 times, “began to” 14, sentences opening with “Sometimes” 33 times, and “opportunity” or “opportunities” 60 times, in about 100 pages.
  • Hedges and padding, and advice without a scene.
  • Front-matter slips: a contents heading, a missing full stop and an unnamed copyright holder.

How I worked: four passes, big issues first

  • Developmental read: an editorial letter with chapter-by-chapter notes.
  • Line edit: tracked changes with a reason beside each one.
  • Copyedit: a clean manuscript with consistent style.
  • Proofread and package: a last look before print, plus a full-wrap cover.
What stays yoursI tighten and clarify; I don’t rewrite you. The lines that carried the author’s personality were left exactly as written, on purpose.
