The Book of Eno

A Christian novel taken from a rough draft to a live Kindle book.

A Story of Second Chances

My job
Editing, cover and formatting
Format
6 × 9 in, 75 pages, Kindle eBook
Platform
Amazon KDP
Tools
Canva and MS Word
Delivered
August 2026

What came in

  • A Word manuscript of a Christian inspirational novel set in New Orleans, written in the author’s own street voice.
  • Strong dialogue and characters, but a slow opening, uneven pacing, early reveals, and slips in tense and dialogue formatting.
  • No finished cover, only a rough idea: Eno, the old car, the church.

What I did

  • Developmental feedback: a 24-page review, a Chapter 1 report, then an editorial review of the August draft.
  • Guided the author’s own revisions across those rounds and kept his voice intact.
  • Designed the Kindle cover with him, from his concept.
  • Formatted the interior in Word: title page, contents, author’s note, chapter openers.
  • Prepared the book for Amazon KDP. It is live on Kindle.
The detail that matteredOn Kindle the cover is judged as a small thumbnail, so the title and author name had to stay readable when tiny.
