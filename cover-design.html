What a cover from me includes

  • One wrap: front, spine and back designed together, as one print file.
  • Print specs: set for your trim size, bleed and spine width, with space left for the barcode.
  • Thumbnail test: titles and author names that stay readable at the size of a store thumbnail.
  • Your idea first: I start from your concept, as I did for The Book of Eno: Eno, the old car and the church.
  • Launch mockups: images you can use for launch posts and sales pages.
  • Matches the book: the cover, the interior and the editing come from the same hands.
