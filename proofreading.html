Proofreading for self-publishing authors

The last look before you print or upload. I catch typos, front-matter slips and unfinished sentences, and I flag anything I cannot settle without you.

Get your free sample editSee my work

What proofreading catches

  • Typos and missing punctuation
  • A contents page that does not match your chapters
  • Copyright page problems, including an unnamed copyright holder
  • Unfinished sentences, especially at the end of an introduction
  • Front-matter slips, because the first pages decide how carefully readers expect the rest to be made

What you get

  • A publication-ready file
  • A short list of open questions that need your decision before printing

Proofread first or edit first?

Proofreading comes last. If your structure or sentences still need work, start with developmental editing or a line edit, so you do not proofread text that will change.

Questions about this service

Is proofreading the same as editing?
No. Editing improves structure, sentences and style. Proofreading is the final check for errors after the editing is done.
Can you proofread and format my interior?
Yes. For Finding My Direction I did both interior formatting and proofreading, so the book went from a plain Word file to a designed interior in one process.
