What formatting includes

  • Front matter: title page, copyright page, dedication and a table of contents with dot leaders and page numbers.
  • Chapter openers: a label, a styled title, an ornament or illustration and, where it suits the book, a drop cap.
  • Page numbers: roman numerals for the front matter, then arabic from Chapter 1.
  • Print and Kindle: layouts for Kindle eBooks, paperbacks and hardcovers on Amazon KDP, in your trim size.
  • Bilingual and children’s books: each text block placed beside its illustration, in the same grid on every page.
  • One person from edit to layout: where a contents page or a copyright line needs a decision, I ask while formatting, not after printing.

Before and after: Finding My Direction

By Clara. Interior formatting with proofreading. The manuscript arrived as a Word file with default styles, and left as a designed book interior.

  • Title: was a heading at the top of a shared first page. Now a title page with artwork, subtitle and byline.
  • Copyright: was below the title. Now its own page in smaller serif text.
  • Contents: there was none. Now a table of contents with dot leaders and page numbers.
  • Page numbers: small roman numerals. Now circled folios, roman then arabic.
